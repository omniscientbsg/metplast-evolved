```
### Choose the Right Feeder for Your Farm

Metplast builds feeders in four materials. Each exists for a reason:

ZAM (Zinc–Aluminium–Magnesium) Coated
Our premium coating for feeders and cage wire. Zinc forms the base
corrosion barrier. Aluminium builds a stable protective oxide layer that
resists heat and humidity. Magnesium is the difference-maker — it seals
and protects cut edges and scratches, exactly where ordinary coatings
fail first. In an ammonia-rich poultry shed, ZAM lasts significantly
longer than standard galvanizing.

Aluminium Feeder
Lightweight and naturally rust-free. Smooth surface keeps feed flowing
and makes cleaning fast. Suited to farms that want low weight on the
trolley line and zero corrosion worry.

GI (Galvanized Iron) Feeder
The proven workhorse — strong, economical, and field-tested across
Indian farms for decades. The right choice where budget and durability
must meet.

PVC Feeder
Corrosion-proof, food-safe, easy to wash, and economical. A practical
choice for farms prioritising hygiene and simple maintenance.
```
